    How to read this

    Each highlighted word shows the model's estimate of how many readers in ten would call it biased.

    The estimate comes from MBIC, a dataset in which about ten crowd workers in the US read each of 1,700 news sentences from 2017 to 2020 and marked the words they found biased. The model learned how often those readers agreed, not a single verdict.

    • Readers split marks words that some readers flag and others do not. These words are contested: reasonable readers disagree about them.
    • Most readers would flag it marks words that 6 or more readers in 10 would flag.

    A highlight is a prompt to look again, not a ruling. The model reflects those annotators' judgements of US political news in English. It can miss words that are new or rare, and it can be wrong.

    Privacy: the text is analysed on this board, kept only while it is being analysed, and not sent anywhere.
